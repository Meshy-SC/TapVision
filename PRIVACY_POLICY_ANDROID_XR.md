# TapVision Privacy Policy — Android XR

- **App:** TapVision (on Google Play, for Android XR devices)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 21, 2026

---

## Summary

TapVision has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

**What TapVision collects and processes:** your **TapVision in-app purchase data from Google Play**: the prices of the worlds and solution-credit packs in your currency, which worlds and packs your Google account bought in TapVision, a purchase reference (token) for each purchase, and whether each credit pack has been delivered. The app uses this data only to show prices, unlock what you bought and deliver your credits, and it handles it on your device. Payment itself happens in Google Play's own purchase screen, with your Google account: we never see your payment details, name or email address.

Your game progress (solved levels, stars, credits, settings) is stored on your device, and in Android's backup if you have it turned on. Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data the App Uses and How

### 2.1 Your TapVision purchases (Google Play)

TapVision has ten worlds of 100 levels. The first 10 levels of the Iron world and level 1 of every other world are free. In the app you can buy:

- **World unlocks** (one-time purchases): each of the ten worlds, or all of them at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- **Solution credits** (packs of 1, 5, 10 or 25): one credit plays the solution of one level (the board clears itself in the right order), and playing that level's solution again is then free. New players get one credit for free. Credits can be used inside the game only. They have no value outside it and can never be exchanged for money.

What happens:

- **Payment** happens entirely in Google Play's own purchase screen, with your Google account. We never see or receive your payment details, your name, your email address or your billing address.
- **What the app receives from Google Play, on your device:** the prices of the worlds and credit packs in your currency, the list of worlds and packs your Google account bought in TapVision, and a purchase reference for each one.
- **How it is used:** to open the worlds you own, to add the credits of each pack to your balance exactly once and then tell Google Play that the pack was delivered, and to restore your worlds after a reinstall or on another device. If a purchase is interrupted, the app delivers it the next time it starts, or when you choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**. The app does not send this information anywhere else.

The app talks to Google Play through the Google Play Billing library, which is Google's code inside the app. Google handles purchases, and anything that library reports to Google, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.2 Eyes, hands and controllers

TapVision is a window in your space. Android XR turns where you look and pinch (or point with your hand or a controller) into taps on that window, as on a touch screen: the app only receives where on its window you point, press and drag, so that you can tap tiles and press buttons. This is used on the spot and is never saved or sent. The app does not read where your eyes look, your head position, your hands, your room or any camera image: Android XR keeps those to itself.

### 2.3 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

To keep your game going, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

- The levels you have solved, your best stars on each, and the level you last played
- Whether you have finished the guided tutorial and seen the two gameplay tips
- Your solution credits, and the levels whose solution you unlocked with them
- The worlds your Google account owns, as last confirmed by Google Play, so that they open without a connection
- The references of the credit packs already delivered, so that a pack is never added twice
- Your music and sound settings

**Android backup.** If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this saved game in the backup Google keeps for you, so that it can be restored when you install TapVision again, on the same device or another one. Google runs that backup under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Progress, worlds and credits in TapVision on other platforms are separate: nothing is shared between them.

## 4. Crash Reports

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If the app crashes and you have allowed your device to share diagnostics with Google, Google Play Console shows us the technical details: the device model, the Android and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened. We use them only to fix the problem.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your device

You can delete it yourself, immediately: **uninstalling TapVision**, or clearing its storage in the device's settings, permanently deletes all of the app's data from your device. If Android backup is on, you can delete TapVision's backup in your Google account's backup settings, or it can be restored when you install the app again.

### Data held by Google

Your purchases are part of your Google account. You can manage or delete the data Google holds about your account with Google's own tools, described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We hold no copy of this data.

### Ask us

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**. Within **30 days**, we delete any emails you have sent us, help you remove the data described above, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

We will not refuse a deletion request. No law requires us to keep any of this data.
