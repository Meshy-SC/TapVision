# TapVision Support

Welcome to TapVision Support. We are here to help you get the most out of your tile puzzle
experience.

## Supported Platforms

TapVision is available on the following Apple platforms:

- **iOS 26+** — iPhone
- **iPadOS 26+** — iPad
- **macOS 26+** — Mac
- **visionOS 26+** — Apple Vision Pro

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Tap tiles to slide them off the board. Each tile moves in its arrow direction.
Clear all tiles to complete the level. Obstacles like gears, teleporters, hooks,
and rotation centers add complexity as you progress.

**How many levels are there?**
TapVision features 1,000 puzzles across 10 difficulty worlds: Iron, Bronze, Silver,
Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Emerald, Master, Grandmaster, and Legend.

### Stars & Scoring

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Complete a level to earn 3 stars. Stars are awarded upon clearing all tiles from
the board.

**How does scoring work?**
Your score increases with each tile you clear. The total score tracks your
cumulative progress across all levels.

### Controls

**iPhone & iPad**
- Tap a tile to slide it in its arrow direction.
- If blocked by an obstacle or another tile, the tile bounces back.

**Mac**
- Click a tile to slide it in its arrow direction.
- If blocked, the tile bounces back to its original position.

**Apple Vision Pro**
- Look at a tile to highlight it.
- Tap to slide the tile.
- Use the toolbar ornament for navigation, restart, and solution controls.

### Troubleshooting

**The app is crashing or not responding.**
1. Force-quit the app and reopen it.
2. Ensure your device is running the latest OS version.
3. Update TapVision to the latest version from the App Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).

**No sound.**
- Check that your device is not in Silent Mode.
- Verify that Music and Sound Effects are enabled in the in-game Settings.

**A level seems impossible.**
Every level has a verified solution. Try tapping tiles in a different order.
You can also use the Solution button in the toolbar to see the correct tile order.
