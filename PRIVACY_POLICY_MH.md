# TapVision Privacy Policy — Meta Quest

- **App:** TapVision (on the Meta Horizon Store)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 21, 2026

---

## Summary

TapVision is free to download and sells world unlocks and solution credits through the Meta Horizon Store. To do this, **TapVision collects and processes the following user data** through two Meta Horizon platform features:

- your **Meta user ID** for this app (Meta's "User ID" feature)
- your **TapVision in-app purchase data**: which worlds you unlocked, which solution-credit packs you bought, and whether each pack has been delivered (Meta's "In-app purchases and/or downloadable content" feature)

TapVision uses this data only to show you the prices, let you buy worlds and credit packs, unlock what you bought and deliver the credits. It also checks with Meta that your copy of the app came from the Meta Horizon Store. Your game progress (solved levels, stars, credits, settings) is stored on your headset.

Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time, free of charge. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

### 2.1 Your Meta user ID (Meta "User ID" feature)

- **What it is:** the number Meta uses to identify your Meta account inside TapVision. It is not your username. Meta gives each app a different number for the same person, so this one cannot identify you in other apps.
- **How it is processed:** TapVision processes your user ID through the Meta Horizon Platform SDK every time it asks Meta for the prices of its add-ons, opens Meta's checkout, checks your purchases, or marks a credit pack as delivered. Meta uses it to find and record your purchases for your Meta account.
- **What it is used for:** only for in-app purchases and the ownership check (section 2.3). It is not used for advertising, profiling or anything else, and it is not shown in the app.

### 2.2 Your TapVision purchases (Meta "In-app purchases and/or downloadable content" feature)

TapVision has ten worlds of 100 levels. The first 10 levels of World 1 and level 1 of every other world are free. In the app you can buy:

- **World unlocks** (one-time purchases): each of the ten worlds, or all of them at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- **Solution credits** (packs of 1, 5, 10 or 25): one credit plays the solution of one level (the board clears itself in the right order), and playing that level's solution again is then free. New players get one credit for free. Credits can be used inside the game only. They have no value outside it and can never be exchanged for money.

What happens:

- **What is collected:** the prices of the add-ons in your currency, the identifiers (SKUs) of the worlds and credit packs you buy, and whether each credit pack has been delivered.
- **How it is used:** to unlock the worlds you bought, to add the credits of each pack to your balance exactly once, and then to tell Meta that the pack was delivered. If a purchase is interrupted, the app delivers it the next time it starts, or when you choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
- **Payment** happens entirely in Meta's own checkout, run by Meta. Your payment details, name, email address and billing address stay with Meta.

### 2.3 Ownership check (entitlement)

When the app starts, it asks Meta Horizon OS whether your Meta account is entitled to TapVision. It gets back a yes or no answer, which is used only for this check.

### 2.4 Controllers and hands

TapVision is a window (a "panel") in your headset. Meta Horizon OS turns your controller or hand pointer into taps on that window, as on a touch screen: the app only receives where on its window you point, press and drag, so that you can tap tiles and press buttons. This input is used only while you play.

### 2.5 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the app stores the following in its private storage on your headset:

- The levels you have solved, your best stars on each, and the level you last played
- Whether you have finished the guided tutorial
- Your solution credits and the levels whose solution you unlocked with them
- The worlds you own, as last confirmed by Meta, so that they open without a connection
- Your music and sound settings
- The identifiers of the credit packs already delivered, so that a pack is never added twice

**Meta cloud backup.** If cloud backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS may include this data in the backup Meta keeps for your Meta account, so that it can be restored when you install TapVision again, on the same headset or another one. Meta runs that backup under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

Progress, worlds and credits on TapVision for Apple devices are separate: nothing is shared between the two.

## 4. Crash Reports

If the app crashes, Meta Horizon OS may report it to Meta, and Meta's developer dashboard then shows us the technical details: the headset model, the Meta Horizon OS and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened. We use them only to fix the problem.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your headset

You can delete it yourself, immediately: **uninstalling TapVision** permanently deletes all of the app's data from your headset. If cloud backup is on, turn it off for TapVision in your headset's settings first, or the backup can restore it when you install the app again.

### Your user ID and purchase data

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**, from any email address, and tell us which data you want deleted. Within **30 days** we delete all data we hold about you, including any emails you have sent us, guide you through deleting the data stored on your headset and in Meta's cloud backup, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

Your user ID and purchase records are also part of your Meta account. You can delete them, together with your Meta account data, with Meta's own tools described in [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

We will not refuse a deletion request. No law requires us to keep any of this data.
