# TapVision Support — Android XR

TapVision is a tile-tapping puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** clear every tile off the board.
TapVision opens as a window in your space. Point at a tile with your hand (or look at it, or use a controller) and pinch.

- **Pinch a tile:** each tile travels only the way its arrow points. If its path is clear, it slides off the board.
- **Blockers:** a tile that runs into another stops against it and stays there; a tile already touching the one in its way bounces back, and that tap does not count. Finding the order that clears the board is the puzzle.
- **Stars and score:** every level has a known solution, and its number of taps is the level's par. Clearing the board in par taps or fewer earns three stars (10 points), within 20 % over par two stars (7 points), otherwise one star (5 points). The Taps gauge counts your taps. Your best result on each level counts.
- **Undo** takes back your last tap; **Restart** starts the level again. There is no timer and no move limit.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb plays the solution, tapping the tiles in the right order, then starts the level again for you to play. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; replays of that level are free. Watching it earns no stars. Pinch the lightbulb again while it plays to pause it.
- A short guided tutorial runs the first time you open the game; **How to Play → Replay Tutorial** runs it again.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, from a dozen tiles to boards of 56.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, levels open as you solve them: one more level opens for each level of that world you have solved.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, TapVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in TapVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install TapVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels, stars and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
