# Privacy Policy for TapVision

**Last Updated:** April 10, 2026

## Overview

TapVision ("the App") is a tile puzzle game developed by Meshy.
We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we
handle your data.

## Data Collection

TapVision does not collect, store, or transmit any user data to our servers. We have no servers,
no databases, and no access to your information.

### We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or tracking
- Advertising identifiers
- Any data whatsoever

## Data Storage

All your data remains entirely under your control:

1. **Locally on your device**

Game progress — including level completions, scores, and settings — is
stored using Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). All data stays on your device.

We have zero access to your data.

## Third-Party Services

TapVision does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, or tracking
services. The App uses only Apple first-party frameworks.

## Data Sharing

We do not:

- Sell your data
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising
- Track your behavior

## Children's Privacy

TapVision does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13. It is suitable for all ages and complies with COPPA and similar
regulations worldwide.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information to
access, correct, or delete. You can delete all local data by removing the app
from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated"
date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
